Why Spade, the proof, and the test

Four sets for buyers who already know they need better merchant data, ordered from earliest to closest to buying. Key messages carry the lines Spade already stands behind at events. Why Spade makes the case against the alternatives: a lookup against a verified record, a confident match or none, a graph that knows how businesses relate, and one merchant on every rail. The ask invites a test: send two to four weeks of real transactions under NDA, Spade runs them, and you get a field-by-field match report with a walkthrough from the enrichment team.
